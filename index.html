DJ Math Lab · Step 1

Find the heartbeat of the song 🎧

What to do

  1. Press ▶ Play. Every click you hear = 1 beat.
  2. Watch the counter go up. The pink dot is the “1” of each group of 4.
  3. Tap along with the TAP button (or your space bar).
  4. When the timer hits 10 seconds, remember the number on the counter!

Your answers

1. How many beats did the counter show at 10 seconds?

2. BPM means beats per minute. A minute is 60 seconds. How many 10-second chunks fit in a minute?

3. Use your answers to estimate the song’s BPM.

💡 Write down your BPM — you’ll need it in Step 2!
