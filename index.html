DJ Math Lab · Step 1

Find the heartbeat of the song 🎧

What to do

  1. Press ▶ Play. Every click you hear = 1 beat.
  2. Watch the counter go up. The pink dot is the “1” of each group of 4.
  3. Tap along with the TAP button (or your space bar).
  4. Messed up? Press ↺ Reset to count again.
  5. When the timer hits 10 seconds, remember the number on the counter!

🤔 What does BPM mean?

You already know MPH from driving! The word PER means ÷ divide.

🚗 MPH= mileshours 60 MPH = 60 miles in 1 hour
🥁 BPM= beatsminutes 148 BPM = 148 beats in 1 minute

Miles Per Hour  →  Beats Per Minute. Same idea! 🎉

⚠️ But we only count for 10 seconds, not a whole minute… so we’ll need to scale it up!
